    Frequently asked questions

    How long does laundry service take?

    Standard wash, dry, and fold orders are usually ready within 24 to 48 hours. Timing may vary for bulky items or during busy periods.

    Can I request a pickup?

    Yes. Choose Pickup when scheduling, then check that your phone number and pickup address in Settings are up to date.

    Is there a minimum order?

    Per-kilogram services have a 3 kg minimum. Bulky pieces and add-on services are priced separately.

    How should I request special garment care?

    Add your care instructions when scheduling or contact the shop before your order so the team can confirm what is possible.

    Where can I check my order status?

    Open History in the sidebar to review your scheduled and past orders.
